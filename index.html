Privacy Policy for Barbell.calc

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Barbell.calc ("the app") is a barbell plate and percentage calculator. It is an independent app by an NTR Barbell athlete, made with the gym's permission. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

Barbell.calc is free, does not require an account, does not collect your personal information, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. The app collects no data at all — nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Calculator data stays on your device

Everything you enter into the barbell calculator, reverse calculator, and percentage calculator — target weights, unit preferences, plate availability, training max — is stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of it.

No purchases

Barbell.calc is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions, so the app never handles payment information of any kind.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Barbell.calc does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any cross-app/cross-site tracking. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track you.

Children's privacy

Barbell.calc is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised policy at this same URL.
